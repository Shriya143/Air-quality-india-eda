# Air-quality-india-eda
# Air Quality Analysis of Indian Cities (2015-2020)

Exploratory data analysis (EDA) of daily air quality data from 26 Indian cities.

## Dataset
Air Quality Data in India (2015-2020), city_day.csv, from Kaggle.
24,850 daily records after cleaning.

## Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

## What I did
- Cleaned the data (removed duplicates, handled missing values)
- Created Year, Month and Season columns
- Analyzed AQI by city, year and season
- Studied how pollutants relate to AQI
- Compared air quality during the 2020 COVID lockdown with 2019

## Key findings
- Ahmedabad has the highest average AQI (about 452), followed by Delhi, Patna, Gurugram and Lucknow
- Winter is the most polluted season (average AQI about 221), and Monsoon is the cleanest (about 116)
- CO and PM2.5 have the strongest link with AQI
- Average AQI fell from about 213 in 2015 to about 114 in 2020
- AQI dropped about 42% during the 2020 lockdown compared to the same period in 2019
